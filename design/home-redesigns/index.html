Home page redesigns

Five directions for redesigning the home page, built from the current app's design tokens (colors, fonts). Open any card to view a static preview. Pick the one you like — we can wire it into the real Vue component next.

Option 1

Vertical Card Stack

A refined version of today's swipe feed: rounded card, topic chips up top, and a progress rail on the side.
Option 2

Magazine Grid

Trades the one-at-a-time feed for a browsable grid with a large featured story, closer to a news homepage.
Option 3

Split Hero

Full-bleed featured article on the left, a scrollable "up next" queue on the right. Desktop-first.
Option 4

Minimalist Reader

Distraction-free, serif typography, centered column. Leans into the "reading" experience over browsing.
Option 5

Dashboard

Stats strip, article-of-the-day banner, trending list, and topic breakdown — an overview-first home page.

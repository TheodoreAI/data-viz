The Library That Never Burned

Most retellings of the Library of Alexandria end in one dramatic fire, but the archaeological record tells a slower story: centuries of underfunding, shifting imperial priorities, and quiet neglect did far more damage than any single blaze.

By the time the last scholars left, the collection had already been scattered across smaller libraries in Antioch, Pergamon, and Constantinople — a diaspora of knowledge rather than a single catastrophe.

About Data Viz

A personal dashboard for exploring data day to day — part reading app, part trend tracker.

  • Home

    Browse random Wikipedia articles by topic, swipe through them, and save the ones worth keeping.

  • Trending

    See what's rising right now across Wikipedia, Hacker News, GitHub, npm, Cargo, and more.

  • Essays

    A small space for longer-form writing.

  • Collection

    Everything saved from Home lives in your profile, ready to revisit or remove.
